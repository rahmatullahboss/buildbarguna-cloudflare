## 2024-06-01 - Icon-only button accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like `X` for close) require explicit `aria-label` and `title` attributes (using the Bengali string "বন্ধ করুন") and explicit keyboard focus styles (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`) to ensure accessibility for screen readers and keyboard navigation.
**Action:** Always ensure icon-only buttons have proper aria-labels, titles, and focus states across the app.
